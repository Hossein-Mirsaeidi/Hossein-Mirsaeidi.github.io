I am an M.Sc. student in Aerospace Engineering (Dynamics and Control) at Sharif University of Technology, Tehran, Iran. My research interests are control theory, safe control, multi-agent systems, robotics, and intelligent systems.

My M.Sc. thesis, "Safe Intelligent Deadlock Resolution in Cluttered Environments for Multi-Agent Systems," works on control barrier functions and heuristic deadlock resolution methods to maintain collision-free motion while ensuring liveness in safety-critical multi-robot systems.

Research
======
My work spans control barrier functions and safety-critical control, multi-agent systems and deadlock resolution, safe reinforcement learning (training Soft Actor-Critic policies on top of a CBF safety filter), nonlinear control (sliding mode, backstepping, feedback linearization), and adaptive control (adaptive CLF/CBF quadratic programs with concurrent learning, MRAC). I work with the Crazyflie 2.1 platform with Lighthouse positioning, and with PX4 for outdoor drone swarms, as a Graduate Research Assistant at the CNAV Lab, Department of Aerospace Engineering, Sharif University of Technology.

See the [Publications](/publications/) and [Portfolio](/portfolio/) pages for details on my papers and projects.
